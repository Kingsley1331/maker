Click empty space to spawn a shape, or drag to set its size. With the polygon tool, click to add points and double-click to finish (at least 3 points). Drag and release a shape to throw it. While paused, press a shape to select it and drag to move it; drag the corner handles to resize and the top knob to rotate (hold Shift to snap). Click empty space or press Escape to deselect.
