How to use

  • Spawn: click empty space to add the selected shape, or drag to set its size. Click the highlighted shape button again to deselect it so clicks on the canvas will not spawn anything.
  • Spray: with a circle, rectangle, triangle, pentagon, or hexagon selected, turn Spray on and drag to stamp tiny copies along the cursor. Set defaults in the Spray panel; Rand picks that property at random in the min–max range (two colours for colour) for each stamp.
  • Cut: pick a circle, rectangle, triangle, pentagon, hexagon, box, or polygon as the cutter. While paused, click a body to choose which one to cut, then drag fully inside it to punch a hole. Click another body to change the target. The cut is ignored if it overlaps the outline or misses that body.
  • Chain outline: pick a circle, rectangle, triangle, pentagon, hexagon, box, or polygon, turn Chain on, then click or drag as usual. The result is a static closed outline that other shapes collide with, instead of a filled body. Spray and Cut turn off when Chain is on.
  • Box tool: press empty space and drag to the opposite corner; the rectangle is created on release.
  • Walls tool: same corner-to-corner drag as Box, but the result is a hollow rectangle of four walls. Set wall thickness in the Walls panel.
  • Edge tool: press empty space and drag to draw a static line that other shapes collide with. Click to place a short horizontal edge.
  • Polygon tool: click to add points and double-click to finish (at least 3 points).
  • Chain tool: click to add points and double-click to finish (at least 2 points). The result is a static connected line that other shapes collide with.
  • Throw: while playing, drag a shape and release to throw it.
  • Edit while paused: press a shape to select it and drag to move it. Drag the corner handles to resize and the top knob to rotate (hold Shift to snap to 15°).
  • Groups: shapes connected by joints select together. Click a shape in the group (without dragging) to edit it alone; click it again to reselect the group.
  • Deselect: click empty space, press Escape, or click a selected ungrouped shape again. Click the highlighted shape or joint button to turn that tool off.
  • Box select: with no shape tool selected and the simulation paused, drag on empty canvas to draw a dashed box. Every shape the box overlaps is selected as a group (move, resize, rotate, duplicate, or delete together). Click empty space to clear.
  • Copy / delete (paused): with a shape or group selected, use Duplicate or Delete in the Selected panel, or press Ctrl+D / Delete. With a motor joint selected, Delete removes that joint.
  • Joints (paused): pick a joint tool. Pin attaches a body to the world at the click. Revolute, rod, weld, and wheel take two clicks on different bodies.
  • Weld and wheel: a weld is a rod that does not allow rotation at the ends. A wheel attaches a spinning hub (second click) to a chassis (first click) with suspension along the line between clicks.
  • Cancel: Escape or a click on empty space cancels a pending second click.
  • Motors and limits: pause and click a pin, revolute, or wheel to set its motor speed; pin and revolute can also be limited in how far they turn.
  • Zoom: select the Zoom tool in View first. Then the mouse wheel zooms toward the cursor, drag pans the view, and the slider and + / - / 0 work (0 resets to 100%).
  • Elasticity: the toolbar slider sets bounce for new shapes, walls, and any shape that has not been overridden. A selected shape's Elasticity field pins that value so later global changes leave it alone. Collisions use the higher of the two objects.
  • Air drag / Friction: Air drag slows motion through the air; Friction affects sliding on contact. Both apply to every shape.
  • Space toggles Pause / Play.
