Click empty space to spawn a shape, or drag to set its size. With the polygon tool, click to add points and double-click to finish (at least 3 points). Drag and release a shape to throw it. While paused, press a shape to select it and drag to move it; drag the corner handles to resize and the top knob to rotate (hold Shift to snap). Click empty space or press Escape to deselect. Pause and pick a joint tool to connect bodies: pin attaches a body to the world at the click; revolute, rod, weld, and wheel take two clicks on different bodies. A weld is a rod that does not allow rotation at the ends. A wheel attaches a spinning hub (second click) to a chassis (first click) with suspension along the line between clicks. Escape or a click on empty space cancels a pending second click.
